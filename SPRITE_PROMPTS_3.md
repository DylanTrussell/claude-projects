## 1. The empty chair — do this one first

We already have Mittens tied to it. Right now she is rescued and the chair
simply vanishes, so the rescue does not land. This is the after frame.

> An empty wooden chair with frayed cut ropes hanging loose off the arms and
> legs, one rope coil fallen on the ground beside it, the chair knocked slightly
> askew, worn timber, a few dark stains. Nothing sitting in it. Side-on three
> quarter view.

Also worth having, same chair: **the ropes still tied but slack and empty, with
the chair tipped over onto its side.**

## 2. The jailer — a VC cat, not a rat

Correction to what I said before. The Mittens rescue happens in the VC tunnel
(`fps.js` level 0), so her captor is one of the Vietnamese cats, the ones in the
green pith helmet with the red star. The rats are not involved down there.

> A lean Vietnamese cat soldier in a green pith helmet with a red star, olive
> uniform, standing over a prisoner with a coiled rope in one hand and a
> cleaver-shaped knife in the other, sneering, tail lashed low. Full body,
> side-on three quarter view.

And the moment before: **the same cat leaning in close with one paw raised to
strike, seen from the side.**

## 3. Both cats, back to back, surrounded

One sprite that sells the whole two-player premise. Title screen, act card,
poster, all of it.

> An orange tabby cat in a red headband and a black cat in a straw conical hat
> standing back to back in the centre, both braced and firing outward in
> opposite directions, one with a minigun and one with an AK-47, muzzle flashes,
> spent shells in the air, olive fatigues, defiant. Symmetrical hero pose.

---

## 4. Scan ship damage states

Everything that takes damage has to visibly react, and right now the ship that
lasers your boat is invulnerable set dressing. Same hull as the one you made,
three states.

> The same alien warship with the rat skull prow, now battle damaged: a blown
> out crater in the mid hull exposing torn pink tissue and shattered brass
> ribbing, green fire licking out of the wound, one tail fin snapped off.

> The same alien warship critically wrecked and listing nose down, trailing a
> thick plume of black smoke and green flame from the underside, hull plates
> peeling away, the skull prow cracked across.

Keep the ship at the same angle and scale in both so they cut cleanly.
