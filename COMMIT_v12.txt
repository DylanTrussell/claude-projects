v12: mobile controls, rail/tunnel art fixes, hit flash, SFX pass

MOBILE (Dylan: "add a 'shoot' button on mobile version bc right now there
isn't one"). There was one -- #tF has been bound to C.FIRE all along. The two
.tgroup rows are 298px each, so justify-content:space-between laid 624px of
buttons across a 390px iPhone viewport: grenade x=382, jump x=452, FIRE x=536,
i.e. 146px past the right edge. Mobile had no fire and no jump at all, not
just a confusing icon. 2x2 grid per cluster under 820px -> all eight fit in
268px, every right edge <= 381 on a 390px viewport, jump+fire on the lower row
where the thumbs rest. tJ/tF relabelled JUMP/FIRE. #touch hidden during films
(it floated over the video at z-index 20, with JUMP directly under SKIP).

CUTSCENES WIDESCREEN ON MOBILE: video + subtitle now share a .cutwrap so the
portrait rotation moves both. Portrait coverage 26% -> 95.8% of screen;
landscape 54.5% -> 81.1%.

SKYRAIDER FLIES FORWARD: skyraider.png is drawn nose-LEFT while the code
assumed a right-facing plane -- the procedural prop-blur ellipse at +pw/2 was
painting a spinning disc onto the TAIL FIN (Dylan's "propellor on the back"),
and the muzzle burst fired at x=330, right of centre, while shots carry
vx:+1050. Mirrored the sprite draw. Also corrects pitch: with left-facing art,
tilt=-0.09 on W was dropping the nose instead of raising it.

DOOR GUN (Dylan: "its like a giant cat on top of the heli, it should just be
one of the cats sticking out the side"). huey_gunner was drawn at gh2=190 over
a helicopter drawn at hh=150 -- a gunner taller than his own aircraft. And
huey_doorgun.png ALREADY contains a straw-hat VC gunner standing in the open
side door, so v11.2 had layered a second, larger, differently-designed cat on
top of him. Overlay deleted; the baked-in gunner's weapon now has a real
tapered barrel pivoting on the same measured muzzle anchor (gx=277 =
140 + 0.783*175.8, which is why the old procedural line used it too).

ENEMY HIT FLASH (asked for twice, previously ignored): enemies blow out white
on hit and red at <=50% health, via a cached source-atop silhouette in
assets.js. Threshold is 50% not 33% deliberately -- rail enemies cap at
hpMax 2, so a 33% cutoff made the red state mathematically unreachable and it
would have shipped as white-only.
KNOWN LIMITATION: rail rats are hp:1 and die on the first bullet, so the flash
cannot show on the most common enemy in the section. Raising rail enemy HP is
a prerequisite for this feature reading in play -- see design/expansion-scope.md.

TUNNEL EXIT: removed the two fps_claws sprites bobbing up on a 420ms cycle
(Dylan: "get rid of the animation of the cat arms floating up just fade to
white and then from white back into the other scene"). fps.js now fades up to
white on the way out and render.js fades back down on the overworld side, so
the two halves join into one continuous move.

CUTSCENE VIDEO URLS: VIDEO_URLS was exported from chunks.js and imported by
NOBODY -- playCutscene hardcoded './assets/video/...'. Production served the
bundled original (md5 1de9cc99) while v11.2's meow splice sat unused on the
CDN (0d0ced0c). That round verified the CDN asset had changed, not which URL
the running game requests. Now wired, confirmed by watching the actual network
request fire. tools/v12_path_audit.mjs exists to catch this class of bug: it
loads the game, logs every URL fetched, and diffs against what chunks.js
declares.

SHOTGUN ART: regenerated idle + reload using fps_pistol as the style
reference. The v11.2 set was three different art styles at once (flat pixel
pistol, photoreal painterly shotgun, thick-ink comic reload) and the reload
frame showed three sleeved forearms and three paws. Backgrounds keyed by
border flood-fill -- both generations returned alpha extrema (255,255), i.e.
painted-in fake transparency -- re-verified at (0,255), and the reload
pixel-diffed against the idle (mean 40.2) to prove it is not a silent no-op.

AUDIO: all 17 SFX mastered to a -3.0 dBFS ceiling with DC offset stripped. The
shipped set measured -12.2 to -32.3 dBFS RMS (a 20 dB spread) with 9 of 17
peaking hotter than the -3 dBFS ear-safety limit and ~9% DC offset on
sfx_meow/sfx_purr (a click on every trigger). 11 regenerated on Higgsfield to
one consistent brief. Three first attempts were unusable and invisible without
measuring: raygun returned -63 dBFS (noise floor), shotgun -26 dBFS, explosion
with its transient at 1.8s of a 2.0s clip. tools/sfx_master.py does the
trimming and levelling; its one-shot cut is restricted to fresh generations
after it ate sfx_screech (2.02s -> 0.09s) and sfx_gore. The regenerated
screech was discarded -- only 160ms of real content -- and the re-mastered
original kept. music_ratpatrol +45% via a per-track trim in assets.js.

Verified: simtest RESULT VICTORY; 17/17 SFX decode through the Web Audio API;
desktop unchanged (no rotation, touch pad display:none, zero console errors);
FIRE tapped with real touch events spawns tracers on canvas (pixel-diff);
production md5-matches local on all seven modules and all 17 audio files.

NOT verified: the new tunnel shotgun sprites were confirmed by URL and by
