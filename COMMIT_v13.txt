MUSIC AFTER THE FILM (Dylan: "music_rock plays when you skip the intro film,
so its something to do with the intro film playing then that playing"). That
asymmetry WAS the diagnosis. SKIP is a click -- a user gesture -- so
ctx.resume() is granted. Letting the film run out calls startGame() from the
video's 'ended' handler with NO gesture anywhere in the stack, so the
AudioContext stays suspended, s.start() runs against a suspended graph, and the
level begins silent. Nothing throws, which is why it never appeared as an
error. music() now records the wanted track and unlockMusic() re-issues it on
the first input of any kind. Also added the crossfade he asked for: the level
track starts under the last 2.5s of the film and rides the film's volume down.
Chose the instrumental coming up under the film rather than the film's audio
continuing into the level -- the film's audio is baked into the video and can't
be looped or ducked once it ends.
Verified: tools/v13_music_check.mjs reproduces the bug (playing=null, hasSrc=
false at 'ended') then confirms recovery on first keypress. RESULT: MUSIC OK.

VERTICAL BULLETS: the snapshot wire format carried only x/y/kind, so every
projectile drew as an axis-aligned horizontal dash regardless of travel --
firing up sent horizontal tracers climbing the screen sideways (Dylan's
screenshot). sim.js now sends the travel angle at index 4 and the renderer
rotates tracers, alien rays, shrapnel and lances. Confirmed in a screenshot.

EXPLOSIONS: the 16-frame blast sheet was gated behind `big`, so ONLY air-support
bombs and boss kills ever got it -- every ordinary rat/ship death fell through
to bare particle circles. That is exactly what Dylan was looking at. Every death
now gets the sheet, with a random mirror and +-12% scale jitter so a firefight
isn't the identical animation on loop, plus a shockwave ring and a white-hot
core. The rail sections (door gun / Skyraider) had their OWN separate explosion
code -- two flat arcs -- which is where the ships he was shooting blew up; that
now plays the same sheet.

FLAMETHROWER: was literally ctx.arc() discs at random colours, both ending on a
crisp boundary. Now a soft radial core that grows and cools along the
projectile's life (age sent at wire index 5), drawn with 'lighter' so
overlapping puffs fuse into one sheet of fire, and every gradient stop ends
fully transparent so there is no edge anywhere. flameCd 55->34ms for a
continuous stream rather than a dotted line of puffs. Flame hitting dirt now
leaves burning ground that keeps damaging what walks through it.

NAPALM: produced one blast and nothing else. Each canister now lays a wide,
long-lived wall of fire through the same system.

CLAWS REPLACE KNIFE (Dylan: "if i have a knife why do i have claws? pick one or
the other, i vote just claws"). fps_knife is no longer drawn anywhere; K is a
heavy claw swipe. "Nothing happens when i strike" had two causes: reach was
shorter than the enemy's own lunge range, so a stalking cat could stand just
outside it, and a connecting swing produced no lasting feedback. Reach and arc
widened, melee() now reports whether it landed, and the paws lunge along the
view axis with a real anticipation-and-thrust shape. Claws stay bloodied for
~2.6s after a hit, tinted via a source-atop silhouette so it stains the claws
rather than painting a rectangle.

TUNNEL READABILITY (Dylan: "you get attacked in the tunnel and its hard to tell
where its coming from"). Directional damage arcs at the screen edge, stored as
WORLD angles so they stay pinned to the attacker as you turn, with dead-ahead at
12 o'clock. Melee damage window 0.7 -> 0.85 so tunnel enemies are a real threat.

TUNNEL VIEWMODEL ARM CUTOFF (his circled screenshots): drawn at vh=340 with
y0 = H - vh, putting the sprite's bottom edge EXACTLY on the screen edge, so the
forearms terminated in mid-air. Now 408 with a deliberate overhang.

HELICOPTER DAMAGE STATES: the Huey had no damage read at all. Now flashes on hit
via the same source-atop tint the enemies use, trails engine smoke below 60%
hull (darker below 30%), judders below 30%, and blows up in three staggered
blasts on death before the replacement comes in.

SPIKE IMPALEMENT: deathKind was set on the player but never serialized, so the
renderer played the same spin-and-flop ragdoll for every death. Now sent at wire
index 19; a punji death drops straight onto the stakes, jolts, sags and bleeds.

WEAPON ART (Dylan: "the same gun shooting different bullets is fucking lazy").
The hero sprite has one rifle baked in, so gatling/flamethrower/raygun changed
only the bullets. Three new weapon sprites generated on Higgsfield and drawn
over the gun hand, swinging skyward with the aim-up pose.

TUNNEL ENEMY ART: rat_blade redrawn with explicit volumetric shading, rim light,
brass armour and steam vents -- Dylan's note was that tunnel enemies are "flat".

MOLTEN CHEESE (Dylan: "yes the rats are here to steal our cheese... you either
poison or blow up their cheese"). A cheese cache caught in a blast or a flame
now goes up, throwing molten cheese over everything nearby. Rats in the splash
are coated and burn down over ~1.6s instead of dying instantly, leaving yellow
decals rather than blood.

MITTENS BEAT: reaching the tunnel exit without him fires a one-shot vignette --
Mittens alone in the dark, shivering under a flickering light, "...mrrow?" --
over an ElevenLabs voiceover of "I knew I forgot something. Mittens! He's down
here somewhere." Built in-engine rather than as a generated film because it has
to fire WHEN the player wanders out, which a pre-rendered video can't do.

ART PIPELINE: all four new sprites were generated on a PURE BLACK background and
keyed to alpha locally (tools/key_black.py) instead of asking for transparency.
All four came back with alpha extrema (255,255) -- fake transparency, the
standing gotcha -- and keyed cleanly to (0,255).

AUDIO: vo_mittens scaled to an exact -3.0 dBFS peak in the float domain. ffmpeg's
alimiter plus mp3 encoding kept overshooting to +0.2 dBFS, so the gain
arithmetic is done explicitly. Final: peak -2.91, DC -0.000006.
